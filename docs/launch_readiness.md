# Phase 6 Launch Readiness Plan

## Goal

Prepare RELI for staged mainnet launch with explicit legal, security, governance, and operations gates.

## Launch Tracks

1. Regulatory and legal readiness
2. Tokenomics and governance finalization
3. Contract and economic security assurance
4. Operational launch runbook and incident readiness
5. Ecosystem and distribution rollout

## Stage Gates

1. Legal gate
- Jurisdictional legal opinion complete
- Terms, disclosures, and policy documents published

2. Security gate
- Independent smart-contract audit passed
- Independent economic audit passed
- Bug bounty completed with closure report

3. Operations gate
- Treasury multisig configured
- Incident drills passed
- Testnet and canary rollout completed

4. Governance gate
- Timelock activation delays enabled
- Emergency rollback procedure validated

5. Market gate
- Launch communications package complete
- Distribution and liquidity plan approved

## Launch Sequence

1. Private testnet
2. Public testnet
3. Guarded canary mainnet
4. Open mainnet launch

## Phase 6 Exit Criteria

- All launch gates pass in readiness report.
- No unresolved critical incidents in launch window.
- Governance and treasury controls validated in drills.
