# Compliance and Regulatory Checklist (Phase 6)

## Legal and Regulatory

1. Legal entity and jurisdiction mapping complete.
2. Legal opinion per launch jurisdiction complete.
3. Terms of service and risk disclosures published.

## AML/KYC and Sanctions

1. AML/KYC policy documented and approved.
2. Sanctions screening workflow validated.
3. Record retention and escalation procedures documented.

## Data Handling

1. Privacy policy published.
2. Data classification and retention controls documented.
3. Breach-notification workflow documented.

## Tax and Reporting

1. Tax counsel review complete.
2. Required transactional reporting controls documented.
3. Accounting controls for treasury operations validated.

## Operational Readiness

1. Incident response ownership defined.
2. Security contact channel and triage process published.
3. Post-incident review and corrective action workflow defined.
