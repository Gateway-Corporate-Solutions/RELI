# Tokenomics and Monetary Policy (Phase 6 Draft)

## Scope

This document defines launch policy defaults for RELI token economics.

## Policy Parameters

- Fixed genesis supply with explicit treasury allocation ledger.
- Staking requirement for worker participation.
- Slashing penalties for proven fraud and repeat low-quality outcomes.
- Reward allocation tied to verified quality signals.

## Governance Controls

- Parameter updates must pass governance vote.
- Activation delay enforced before policy changes become active.
- Emergency rollback path required for critical regressions.

## Transparency Requirements

- Public parameter registry with change history.
- Public slashing and settlement event trails.
- Public quarterly economics report.

## Launch Policy Checklist

1. Supply and treasury allocation signed off.
2. Emissions or unlock schedule signed off.
3. Slashing and reward policy signed off.
4. Governance update constraints signed off.
